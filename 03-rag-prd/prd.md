## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Hybrid

Hybrid RAG. Use modular RAG to retrieve targeted evidence from separate sources (customer feedback, product docs, analytics, engineering, and strategy), combined with long-context retrieval when Juno needs to synthesize multiple relevant documents. This provides the right evidence for each task, keeps retrieval efficient as the knowledge base scales, and reduces the risk of overwhelming the LLM with irrelevant context.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Juno must access PRDs/product docs, customer feedback (support tickets, interviews, surveys), product analytics, engineering documentation, and company strategy/OKRs. For the MVP, use roughly 12 months of recent data, plus the current/active product documentation. This provides enough volume to identify recurring customer and product patterns while keeping the knowledge base relevant, reducing stale information, and controlling retrieval cost and latency.
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword)
- **Grounding rule:** Juno must provide inline citations with short evidence snippets and clickable source links for every key claim or recommendation. Users should be able to trace each insight back to the original internal source, with clear source name and date, so PMs can quickly verify the evidence and trust Juno’s recommendations.
- **Freshness:** Customer feedback, support tickets, and product analytics should sync daily; active PRDs, roadmap, OKRs, and strategy documents should sync within 1 hour of changes. Engineering and historical documentation can sync weekly. This keeps Juno’s recommendations grounded in current customer behavior and business priorities while avoiding unnecessary real-time infrastructure for slower-changing data.

| # | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
