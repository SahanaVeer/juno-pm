## Autonomy level

Juno can analyze evidence and draft PRDs, recommendations, and outreach; it cannot publish, send, modify source systems, or make product decisions without human approval.

## Controls

- **Kill switch:** max_steps = 8; abort on repeated actions or insufficient evidence after 2 retrieval attempts.
- **Rate / cost caps:** corpus.retrieve → {results:[{text, source, relevance_score}]}
transcript.parse → {insights:[{theme, evidence, source}]}
prd.generate → {draft:{...}, evidence:[{claim, source}]}

Tools return structured JSON with explicit evidence, source, relevance/confidence, and empty-result states so the agent can reason only from verified observations.
- **Escalate-on-stuck:** If a loop breaks or a confidence gate triggers, stop autonomous execution, preserve the evidence gathered, clearly flag the uncertainty, and return control to the PM with a concise explanation and recommended next step.

## Monitoring

**Confidence thresholds (map to actions):**

≥80%: proceed; 60–79%: proceed with a review flag; <60%: stop and require human review.

**Checkpoints:**

Trigger human intervention for high-risk, high-value, or ambiguous decisions; stop and require PM approval when evidence is insufficient, confidence falls below 60%, or the action could materially impact users, product decisions, or source systems.

**North Star (re-read every loop):**

Always ground every insight and recommendation in retrieved evidence, prioritize user needs and product goals, and never invent unsupported information.

## Permissions

Juno can READ approved transcripts, tickets, emails, and product documents; it can WRITE only to draft PRD outputs. It cannot modify source data, send messages, or write to Jira, CRM, or production systems.
