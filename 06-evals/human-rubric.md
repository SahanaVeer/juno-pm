## What graders score

- **Task / product:** Juno PM — AI Associate PM that synthesizes product inputs (meeting transcripts, tickets, emails, and strategy documents) into evidence-backed, structured product insights and PRD drafts.
- **Reviewer audience:** Product Managers, product operations/strategy stakeholders, and trained human evaluators who understand PRD quality and can assess whether Juno’s outputs are accurate, evidence-backed, complete, and useful.
- **Value proposition:** Juno must turn fragmented product inputs into accurate, evidence-backed, actionable insights and a high-quality PRD draft—helping PMs reduce synthesis time while maintaining traceability to the original source material.

_Full 1-5 anchors:_

### 1. Accuracy & Evidence Grounding - Insights and PRD content accurately reflect the source material and are traceable to supporting evidence.

- **Score 1:** Unacceptable: Output is largely inaccurate or unsupported; contains major contradictions, fabricated details, or conclusions not found in the source material. Little to no traceability to evidence.
- **Score 2:** Poor: Output contains some accurate information but has significant inaccuracies, unsupported claims, or misinterpretations. Evidence links are incomplete or frequently unclear.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable: Output is mostly accurate and grounded in the source material, with minor inaccuracies or unsupported inferences. Key claims are generally traceable to evidence.
- **Score 4:** Strong: Output is highly accurate and well grounded. Claims and recommendations are consistently supported by relevant source evidence, with only minor gaps or interpretation.
- **Score 5:** Excellent: Output is fully accurate, evidence-backed, and highly traceable. Clearly distinguishes facts from inference, introduces no unsupported claims, and preserves the meaning and context of the source material.

### 2. Relevance & Strategic Alignment - Output focuses on the most important user/product needs and aligns with the stated product strategy and goals.

- **Score 1:** Unacceptable - Output is largely irrelevant, misses key user or product needs, and conflicts with the stated product strategy or goals.
- **Score 2:** Poor - Output addresses some relevant needs but focuses on lower-priority issues or contains significant misalignment with product strategy.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable - Output addresses the main user/product needs and is generally aligned with the stated strategy, with some gaps in prioritization or alignment.
- **Score 4:** Strong - Output focuses on the highest-value needs and aligns well with product strategy and goals, with only minor gaps.
- **Score 5:** Excellent - Output consistently prioritizes the most important user and product needs and demonstrates clear, precise alignment with strategy, goals, and intended outcomes.

### 3. Completeness & Clarity - The output captures key requirements, insights, and context without important omissions and is easy for a PM to understand and use.

- **Score 1:** Unacceptable - Output is missing major requirements, insights, or context and is difficult to understand or use.
- **Score 2:** Poor - Output captures some key information but has significant omissions, unclear organization, or confusing language that limits its usefulness.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable - Output captures the main requirements, insights, and context and is generally clear, with some minor omissions or areas needing clarification.
- **Score 4:** Strong - Output is comprehensive, well organized, and easy to understand, with only minor gaps that do not materially affect its usefulness.
- **Score 5:** Excellent - Output is complete, concise, and exceptionally clear, capturing all material requirements, insights, and context in a structure that a PM can immediately understand and use.

### 4. Actionability & PRD Quality - Recommendations and PRD drafts are specific, structured, and actionable enough to move product decisions or execution forward.

- **Score 1:** Unacceptable - Recommendations or PRD content are vague, incomplete, or unusable and do not support meaningful product decisions or execution.
- **Score 2:** Score 2: Poor - Output contains some actionable elements but lacks sufficient specificity, structure, or detail to reliably guide decisions or execution.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable - Output provides reasonably specific and structured recommendations or PRD content that can support product decisions, with some refinement needed.
- **Score 4:** Strong - Output is specific, well structured, and actionable, enabling PMs to make informed decisions and move toward execution with minimal refinement.
- **Score 5:** Excellent - Output is highly specific, structured, and immediately actionable, translating insights into clear product decisions, requirements, and next steps with minimal or no PM rework.

### 5. Reasoning & Appropriate Uncertainty - Juno distinguishes facts from assumptions, avoids unsupported conclusions, and clearly flags ambiguity or missing information.

- **Score 1:** Unacceptable - Output presents unsupported conclusions as facts, fails to distinguish assumptions, and does not acknowledge ambiguity or missing information.
- **Score 2:** Poor - Output occasionally identifies assumptions or uncertainty but frequently makes unsupported inferences or overlooks important ambiguities.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable - Output generally distinguishes facts from assumptions and flags major uncertainties, with some minor gaps or overconfident conclusions.
- **Score 4:** Strong - Output consistently separates facts, assumptions, and inferences, appropriately flags ambiguity, and avoids unsupported conclusions.
- **Score 5:** Excellent - Output demonstrates disciplined reasoning throughout, clearly distinguishes evidence from inference, explicitly identifies uncertainty and missing information, and never overstates what the source material supports.

## Calibration

- **Sampling rule:** Sample 10% of Juno outputs for human evaluation, with a minimum of 20 outputs per evaluation cycle. Prioritize high-risk, low-confidence, and randomly selected outputs to ensure both targeted and representative coverage.
- **Cadence:** Weekly during active development; biweekly once Juno reaches production stability, with an additional evaluation after any major model, prompt, workflow, or rubric change.
- **Graders per item:** 2 independent graders per item, with a third grader used to resolve disagreements that exceed the defined threshold.
- **Calibration cadence:** Calibrate graders weekly during active development, reviewing a shared set of 5–10 sample outputs before each evaluation cycle. Move to monthly calibration once evaluation results show consistent grader agreement.

If graders differ by ≥2 points on any dimension, they must review the source evidence and rubric anchors independently, then discuss to reach consensus. If consensus cannot be reached, a third senior reviewer makes the final determination. All disagreements and resolutions are logged to identify rubric or model weaknesses.

## Pass bar

Overall score ≥4.0/5, with no individual dimension below 3. A score below the threshold triggers human review and targeted improvement before the output is considered production-ready.
